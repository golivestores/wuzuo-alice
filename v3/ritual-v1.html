The Practice

Your First Candle Ritual

☽ ✦ ⚴ ☉
01 🌿

Cleanse Your Space

Light sage, palo santo, or incense. Walk the smoke through the room to clear stagnant energy and create a sacred container for your ritual.

02 📜

Set Your Intention

Write your desire on paper. Be specific — "I want love" is weak. "I invite deep, mutual connection with a partner who honors my spirit" carries power.

03 🫙

Dress the Candle

Anoint your candle with essential oil — rose for love, cinnamon for wealth, frankincense for protection. Stroke from center outward to charge it with your energy.

04 🕯

Light the Flame

As the wick catches, speak your intention aloud three times. Feel the words leaving your body and entering the fire. The flame is now your bridge to the unseen.

05 🧘

Meditate

Sit with the flame for at least ten minutes. Visualize your intention as already accomplished — see it, feel it, believe it. Let the fire carry your vision.

06 ✨

Release

Allow the candle to burn completely. Never blow it out — use a snuffer. The breath that extinguishes the flame disperses the intention you placed within it.

Sacred Geometry

The Art of Sigil Craft

Venus Sigil

Every sigil is born from a Kamea — a magic square of numbers sacred to a planet. The practitioner traces the path of letters across the grid, connecting them into a single continuous line. This line becomes the sigil: a compressed symbol encoding an entire intention.

The Venus sigil shown here is inscribed on every WuZuo Alice love candle. When the candle burns, the wax melts the sigil away — releasing its encoded intention into the flame.
