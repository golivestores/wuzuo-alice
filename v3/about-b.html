Chapter I

The Origin

Every candle began as a prayer.

It started with a fascination — ancient symbols etched into wax by candlelight, each line carrying centuries of intention. Alice spent years studying the sigils of Western Wicca and Eastern Taoist fu-script, searching for the intersection where spiritual traditions converge.

The first WuZuo Alice candle was hand-poured on a personal altar during a new moon, inscribed with a single rune of protection. When friends, then strangers, began asking “where did you get this?” — a brand was born.

From a one-room studio to a full ritual workshop, every candle still begins the same way: with intention, under the moon, by hand.
