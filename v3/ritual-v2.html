Chapter I

The Seven Planetary Seals

Each planet possesses a unique sigil — a geometric key that unlocks its influence. These seals are drawn on parchment, carved into candles, or inscribed upon talismans during the planet's ruling hour.

☉☽♂☿♃♀♄

The seal must be drawn in one unbroken line, beginning at the planet's number on the Magic Square, while reciting the corresponding divine name.

Chapter II

The Planetary Hours

Time is not neutral. Each hour of the day falls under the governance of a celestial body. To cast a spell of love, work in the hour of Venus. For wisdom, Mercury. For banishing, Saturn.

DawnSun → Venus → Mercury
MorningMoon → Saturn → Jupiter
NoonMars → Sun → Venus
AfternoonMercury → Moon → Saturn
DuskJupiter → Mars → Sun
NightVenus → Mercury → Moon

* Sequence begins with the planet ruling the day (e.g., Sunday = Sun first)

Chapter III

The Circle of Art

Before any invocation, the magician must construct the Circle of Art — a sacred boundary that protects the operator and contains the summoned force. The circle is drawn with the point of a consecrated sword, nine feet in diameter.

At each cardinal point, inscribe the name of the ruling archangel:

East — Raphael (Air)
South — Michael (Fire)
West — Gabriel (Water)
North — Uriel (Earth)
Chapter IV

The Art of Invocation

Invocation draws a force inward — into the self, the candle, or the talisman. Unlike evocation (which summons externally), invocation merges the magician's will with the planetary intelligence.

"I invoke thee, O great and mighty ☉ SOL, by the sacred names IAO, ABRAXAS, and SABAO. Fill this vessel with thy golden light. Let thy power flow through this flame as it burns."

Light the candle as you speak the final word. The flame is the bridge between worlds.
